# FINANCIAL_ENGINE — v15 (honest status)

**Status: no_live_feed.** Revenue, profit, input costs, cash flow, break-even,
insurance exposure, and loan readiness all require the farmer's real **prices and
costs**, which are not wired into the platform.

So the Financial Engine is NOT built to emit numbers — and the Farmer Copilot
**declines** profit questions at confidence 0, explaining that it won't guess and
pointing the farmer to log sales + costs and consult an advisor or cooperative.

A fabricated profit or loan-readiness figure could lead a farmer into a bad
financial decision — which is exactly why the honest answer is "I don't know yet,
here's how to find out." The real path: a price feed + a cost ledger the farmer
maintains, then arithmetic (not AI) over real numbers.
