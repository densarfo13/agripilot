# WORLD_CLASS_REPORT — Farroway v13

## The honest definition of "world-class"
A world-class agricultural intelligence platform is not one that prints a
confident number for everything asked of it. It is one that **knows what it knows,
says what it doesn't, and never fabricates the difference.** By that definition,
v13 advances the platform meaningfully:

- **Built real:** a living Digital Twin of the whole farm hierarchy, and an
  autonomous Farm Agent that produces an evidence-cited morning plan from real
  signals. These make the platform feel like an invisible expert — and they are
  honest.
- **Declared truthfully:** every other v13 ask (250k species, satellite, drone,
  disease/pest prediction, market AI, carbon, biodiversity, millions-of-farms
  infra) is mapped to its real status with the capability it would require. None
  is faked.

## Why the gaps are a feature, not a failure
Each `requires_cv` / `no_live_feed` / `requires_validation` / `requires_infra`
entry is a precise, fundable next step — a vision model, a market feed, a satellite
provider, an infra program, the live pilot. The platform becomes more capable by
LIFTING these one at a time with real data, not by pretending they're done.

**This is the invisible agricultural expert, honestly: it advises from evidence,
and when it cannot, it says so — because unknown is always allowed.**
