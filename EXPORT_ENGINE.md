# EXPORT_ENGINE — v15 (honest status)

**Status: advisory.** Export readiness, traceability, compliance, quality,
packaging, and cold-chain readiness are honest **checklists against documented
criteria** — not a fabricated certificate, score, or pass/fail the platform can't
actually verify.

What's genuinely buildable next (and named in the v14 registry as the most honest
real feature): a **traceability chain-of-custody record** (farm → harvest → packing
→ transport → retail) with a verifiable QR. That is real, non-predictive data
plumbing — no model, no feed, no fabrication. Until built, export readiness stays an
advisory checklist, clearly labelled as guidance rather than certification.
