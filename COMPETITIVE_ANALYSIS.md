# Competitive Analysis

*Basis: model knowledge of these companies (not live market research — commission a real landscape
study before fundraising). Positions are directional.*

| Competitor | Their strength | Farroway vs them |
|---|---|---|
| **Plantix** (closest) | Massive scan volume, trained CV models, agri-input marketplace (India) | They win on model accuracy today (real trained models). Farroway wins on breadth-beyond-scan (tasks/timeline/market/NGO/gov in one loop) + honesty architecture. |
| **Farmerline** (Ghana — home turf) | Voice/SMS reach, farmer networks, W. African distribution | Their distribution is the asset to partner with, not fight. Farroway's PWA + offline + Twi/Hausa localization is competitive on product. |
| **Apollo Agriculture** (Kenya) | Input financing at scale, credit models from satellite+ML | They are finance-first; Farroway's consent-gated finance seam could plug into such a lender rather than compete. |
| **CropIn / DeHaat** (India) | Enterprise agristack, gov/agribusiness contracts; DeHaat full-stack rural retail | Enterprise sales muscle. Farroway's tenant-isolated NGO/gov portals are comparable tech at pilot scale. |
| **Climate FieldView / John Deere Ops / FarmLogs / AgriWebb** | Precision ag for mechanized large farms (equipment telemetry, agronomic analytics) | Different segment — not Farroway's smallholder market. Low collision. |
| **OneSoil** | Free satellite field monitoring | Satellite is their core; Farroway honestly has no feed yet — integration candidate. |

## Weaknesses (honest)
No trained models (identification rents plant.id) · zero field traction data · one country · no
distribution partnerships · satellite/market feeds absent.

## Advantages
Honesty-by-construction (17 anti-fabrication gates — unique among peers who routinely over-promise
to farmers) · single-brain event architecture (every module feeds one recommendation loop) ·
low-literacy UX (voice/simple-mode/6 locales, 48px, offline-first) · 408-gate engineering
discipline unusual at this stage.

## The moat (if earned)
Not the code — copyable. The moat is the **trust + labeled-outcome flywheel**: farmers who trust
honest advice keep scanning/confirming → proprietary correction/outcome dataset for W. African
smallholder crops → models competitors can't buy. That flywheel starts at pilot farmer #1 and
compounds; it is the single asset worth defending.

## Opportunities
Partner with Farmerline-class distribution; plug lenders into the consent-gated finance seam; NGO
program contracts (portal already built); position as the *honest* alternative in a market burned
by over-promising apps.
