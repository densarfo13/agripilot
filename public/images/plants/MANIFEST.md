# /public/images/plants — full-plant photography

Reserved for full-plant or growing-stage images used by the
Plant Companion / care-tip surfaces. Naming + format rules
match `/public/images/crops` (1:1 WebP, ≤ 80 KB).

The 16 priority slots mirror the crop list — drop
`<key>.webp` files here when the curated plant-stage photos
are available. Until then, components fall back to
`/public/images/placeholders/plant.svg`.
